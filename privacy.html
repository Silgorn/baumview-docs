Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BaumView app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") developed as a file manager tool. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Information Collection and Use

BaumView operates entirely locally on your device. We respect your privacy:

  • We do not collect, store, view, or transfer any of your personal files, documents, or directories.
  • We do not use any external servers or cloud databases to process your data. All file operations (copying, moving, deleting, compressing) are executed locally.

2. Device Permissions

To function properly as a file manager, the Application requires specific device permissions:

  • Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Read/Write permissions): Used exclusively to let you browse, manage, organize, and view files on your internal storage, SD cards, and connected USB/OTG devices.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools that collect personal user data.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via the GitHub repository issues page.
